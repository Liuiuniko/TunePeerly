# Trademark policy

This policy covers the names and logo used to identify official releases of this project. It is separate from the AGPLv3 copyright license. The AGPLv3 license lets others use, modify, and distribute the code. It does not, by itself, let them brand another product as this one.

Writing this policy down does not register a trademark. Whether a name is protected, and how far that protection goes, depends on the applicable law.

## Official marks

The official marks are:

- The name **TunePeerly**
- The Chinese name **声渡**
- The application icon shipped with official releases

An official release is a release published by zhebuilds from the [TunePeerly repository](https://github.com/zhebuilds/TunePeerly).

## What others may do

You may truthfully say that your version is based on TunePeerly, including in an About or Credits screen. Use a phrase such as "Based on TunePeerly" or "基于 TunePeerly".

You may use the name in a description that does not make your version look like an official release. For example: "ExamplePlayer, an independent fork of TunePeerly".

## What needs permission

Do not use TunePeerly, 声渡, or the official icon as the name, logo, or primary brand of a modified version, unless you have prior written permission from zhebuilds.

Do not state or imply that a modified version is official, authorized, endorsed, or supported by zhebuilds.

Do not use zhebuilds as the publisher or sponsor of a modified version.

写下本政策并不等于已经注册商标。一个名称是否受保护、保护范围有多大，取决于适用的法律。
